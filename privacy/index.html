Privacy policy

PRIVACY

This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what you can do about it.

LAST UPDATED: 3 NOVEMBER 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Yoka Projects Ltd, trading as kadmon projects (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us at hello@kadmonprojects.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form:

  • your email address
  • the content of your inquiry, including anything you write about yourself or your project

When you browse the site, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and keep it secure. The site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

We use your details only to read and answer your inquiry, and to continue the conversation if you want to work with us. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

You are not required by law to give us your details. Without an email address, we cannot reply to you.

Who receives it

Your information is handled by our team and by three service providers that we use to run the site and our email:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website
  • Web3Forms, which receives contact form submissions and forwards them to our email
  • Google (Google Workspace), which provides our email, where your inquiry is delivered

These providers process the information on our behalf, under their own security and data protection terms. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Transfers outside Israel

Netlify, Web3Forms and Google store data on servers that may be located outside Israel, including in the United States. By using these established providers, we rely on their contractual and security safeguards to protect your information.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and our correspondence with you for as long as they may be relevant to our business, and we do not delete them on a fixed schedule. You can ask us to delete yours at any time, and we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. To do so, email hello@kadmonprojects.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Privacy Protection Authority in Israel, or the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date above.
